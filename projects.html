eBird Explorer

A trip planner built on eBird’s live data. For each trip, it lists the birds I still need, where they’ve been reported lately, and which hotspots hold the most of them, so a day in the field goes where the birds are. I built it for my own trips, from Alaska and Quebec to Puerto Rico and Oaxaca.

Web app · 2026

Shoot Planner

A planner for landscape photography. Pick a date and a base, and it scores my saved spots for sunrise, sunset, golden light, soft light, fog, stars and aurora from free forecast, tide and space-weather data. Then it builds the day: when to wake, where to drive, and a backup for each session, with the reasons behind every score. It started with a stormy week in Charlevoix, Quebec, in October 2026, and works wherever I travel.

Web app · 2026

Retreat Finder

A finder for places to stay on photography trips. Claude searches Airbnb, VRBO, Booking.com and hosts’ own sites and reads each listing. The app scores every place against my taste: a view from where I sit, good design, a real desk, quiet. It shows the evidence behind each judgement, remembers every place I’ve turned down and why, and tracks prices for exact dates. I built it for my own trips, so it opens to a sign-in.
