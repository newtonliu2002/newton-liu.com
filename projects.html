eBird Explorer

A trip planner built on eBird’s live data. For each trip, it lists the birds I still need, where they’ve been reported lately, and which hotspots hold the most of them, so a day in the field goes where the birds are. I built it for my own trips, from Alaska and Quebec to Puerto Rico and Oaxaca.
