About

I photograph birds and wildlife across North America, with a particular weakness for shorebirds, raptors in bad weather, and any landscape that requires getting up before four. My work is unbaited and uncalled — every frame here is a wild animal behaving as it would have without me.

Gear is a means, not a point, but for the curious: a full-frame body, a 600mm prime for birds, and a 24–70mm for everything the long lens can't step back far enough to hold.
